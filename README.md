**LyricPop**

A client-side mod that shows synced lyrics for whatever song is currently playing on your computer while you're in Minecraft.

It detects the song automatically (Spotify, YouTube, browsers, etc.) and pulls timed lyrics from lrclib.net. You can display them a few different ways:

- A Spotify-style panel you can put in any corner
- Floating popup words that appear on screen
- Lyrics that actually float in the 3D world around you

Pretty much everything is customizable — colors, size, opacity, position, how long the popups last, whether they drift, word-by-word or full line, etc. Press **J** to open the settings page in your browser.

**Notes**
- Windows and macOS work with no extra setup
- Linux needs `playerctl` installed
- Client-side only (Forge 1.21.1)
- Keybinds: Y to toggle lyrics, U to toggle the floating popups, J to open settings

That's pretty much it. Just a fun little mod if you like listening to music while you play.
